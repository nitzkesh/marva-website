# Marva — digital brand book
Version: 2026-10-28. Derived from the approved live site. Companion: Marva_Brand_Book_2026-10-28.pdf.

## Brand and audiences
מרווה / Marva turns a useful physical product into advertising space: advertisers fund the bottle; the audience receives water free. The observed expression is local, direct, urban and approachable, with Tel Aviv Bauhaus imagery.
Advertisers buy attention/distribution. Distribution partners hand out already-funded bottles. Consumers receive the water. Do not collapse those three roles.

## Voice
Use short, concrete sentences and direct benefits. Address the advertiser's campaign, the partner's customers, or the consumer's experience explicitly. Let the object and image explain the model.
Use observed calls to action: צור קשר; להצעת מחיר; אני רוצה לפרסם; אני רוצה לחלק בקבוקים; בואו נדבר.
Do not invent proof, quantified ROI, confirmed events, client endorsements, sustainability certification or print promises from the aesthetic.
The live site is bilingual: Hebrew RTL and English LTR. Keep phone numbers, URLs and codes LTR.

## Logo and imagery
Use the existing production mark at https://marva-water.com/marva-mark.svg; do not redraw it with an image model. Preserve its viewBox, shape and original color relationships. Use the live site's lockup treatment as reference.
The live entry uses a large Hebrew wordmark over Bauhaus photography; the mark also appears as the favicon/footer identity. Different applications may use different treatments.
Do not infer millimetre minimum sizes, legal trademark status or final print clear-space rules from the website. For print, obtain production artwork and supplier specifications.
Visual vocabulary: pale urban architecture, coastal light, sage/sky/sand surfaces, high-contrast black typography, generous space and bottle imagery.
Concept brands/bottle mockups are illustrative. VELMORA is explicitly labelled an example brand on the live page. Do not present concept artwork as a client campaign.
Cinema City and Kapayim are shown in the site's partner section; this records the display, not a new testimonial or permission grant.

## Typography
- Display/headings: Rubik. Live stylesheet request includes weights 400, 500, 700, 900.
- Hebrew body: Assistant first; requested weights 400, 600, 700.
- English family: Work Sans; requested weights 400, 500, 600, 700.
- UI fallbacks: Segoe UI, system-ui, sans-serif.
Use live responsive styles for exact sizing; do not revive the superseded July landing-page scale.

## Motion and components
Motion supports the bottle and spatial story. The live bundle includes GSAP and ScrollTrigger. Keep the readable message independent of animation and respect reduced-motion preferences in future work.
Audience choices, detail accordions, labelled fields, consent checkbox and clear submit buttons form the reusable interaction vocabulary. No submitted-form success is claimed by this documentation.

## Applications
Website first; adapt the same color/typography/imagery to proposals, decks and social templates. Each derivative should name its source date and avoid introducing a competing palette or slogan.
Bottle-label production is a separate manufacturing deliverable. The advertiser page itself distinguishes the advertisement area from mandatory manufacturer/mineral/deposit/recycling content. Do not treat the entire label as unconstrained artwork.
